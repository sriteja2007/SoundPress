# Chrome Web Store Listing — SoundPress

> Last Updated: 2026-10-19

## Store Listing

**Extension Name**  
SoundPress - Keyboard Sound Effects

**Short Description**  
Plays fun sound effects whenever you type in Chrome. Includes 18 bundled meme, game, and procedural sound effects.

**Detailed Description**  
SoundPress brings typewriter-style and meme sound feedback to your keyboard while browsing the web in Google Chrome.

Every time you type on any webpage, SoundPress plays an audio effect—from classic shotgun and cartoon boings to famous meme sounds. It runs purely inside Chrome and turns off automatically the moment you close the browser.

Key Features:
- 18 Included Sound Effects:
  - Procedural Synth: Shotgun, Cartoon Boing, Censor Beep, Dry Fart, Pew Pew, Quack.
  - Memes & Effects: Gunshot J Budden, Apple Pay, Movie 1, Rizz Sound Effect, Wrong Answer, Yes Lara Voice, Shocked, Punch, Maro Jump, Ding, Anime Ahh, 67.
- Multiple Sound Randomizer: Check several sounds to hear a random mix on each keystroke.
- Volume Settings: Choose between Loud, Medium, and Quiet.
- Armed Switch: Instantly mute or re-enable typing sounds with one toggle.
- Non-Intrusive: Runs exclusively inside your open Chrome tabs. No background processes remain on your computer when Chrome is closed.

How to Use:
1. Click the SoundPress icon in the Chrome toolbar.
2. Select your favorite sound or check Multiple sounds for a randomized experience.
3. Start typing in any text box or document on any webpage.
4. Mute anytime by toggling the "Armed" switch in the popup.

Privacy & Safety:
SoundPress only detects the occurrence of key-down events to trigger sound playback. It does not record, log, or transmit any typed text, characters, or browsing data. SoundPress functions completely offline and sends zero data to external servers.
